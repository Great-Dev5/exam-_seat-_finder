Announcements

Important examination information and updates.

Examination Registration

Students are advised to complete their examination registration before the deadline. All students must ensure that their registration is properly done to avoid any issues during the examination period. The registration portal will be open from the 1st of September to the 15th of September, 2026. Students are encouraged to register early to avoid last-minute rush and technical issues that may arise. Any student who fails to register before the deadline will not be allowed to sit for the examination. Ensure all required documents and fees are submitted correctly. For assistance, contact the examination office or visit the student support portal.

Seat Allocation

Examination seats will be published through the Exam Seat Finder portal. All students are expected to check their allocated seats at least 24 hours before the examination date. The seat allocation system ensures that every student is assigned a specific seat in the examination hall to maintain order and prevent overcrowding. Students must sit in their assigned seats as any deviation may result in disqualification. The seat allocation list will be available on the portal on the 5th of September, 2026. In case of any issues with seat allocation, students should report to the examination office immediately.

Examination Rules

Students must follow all examination rules and instructions as provided by the university. The use of mobile phones, smartwatches, or any electronic devices is strictly prohibited during the examination. Students are also required to present their student ID cards and examination dockets before entering the hall. Any form of cheating, impersonation, or misconduct will be treated with zero tolerance and may result in expulsion or other severe penalties. Students must arrive at the examination hall at least 30 minutes before the start of the exam. All bags and personal belongings must be placed at designated areas outside the hall.

Important Notice

Always check the portal for updated examination information. Students are advised to frequently visit the Exam Seat Finder website for any changes or updates regarding their examinations. Notices regarding exam postponements, venue changes, or schedule adjustments will be posted on the portal and announced via the university's official communication channels. It is the responsibility of every student to stay informed about all examination-related matters. Failure to check for updates will be at the student's own risk. For urgent inquiries, please contact the examination office directly.
