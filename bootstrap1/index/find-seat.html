Student Information

How to Find Your Examination Seat

Follow these simple steps to retrieve your assigned examination seat information.

Step 1: Enter Details

Type in your Matriculation Number and Student ID Number exactly as they appear on your student records. Make sure there are no typos or extra spaces.

Step 2: Verify Identity

Our system will verify your information against the university's database. This ensures that only authorized students can access their seat details.

Step 3: View Your Seat

Once verified, your assigned seat number, examination hall, course, date, and time will be displayed on the screen.

What You Will See

Your examination seat information will include the following details.

Seat Number

Your unique seat identifier (e.g., A01, B05, C12)

Examination Hall

The venue where you will sit for your exam

Course Details

The course code and course title for each exam

Date & Time

The exact date and time of your examination

Important Reminders

Check 24 Hours Before

Always verify your seat at least 24 hours before your examination to avoid last-minute issues.

Bring Your ID Card

You must present your student ID card and examination docket at the examination hall.

Arrive Early

Arrive at your examination venue at least 30 minutes before the scheduled start time.

Sit In Your Assigned Seat

You must sit in the exact seat assigned to you. Any deviation may lead to disqualification.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about finding your examination seat.

What if I can't find my seat information?

If your seat information is not showing, please verify that your matriculation number and student ID are correct. If the issue persists, contact the examination office immediately.

Can I change my assigned seat?

No, seat assignments are final and cannot be changed. Students must sit in their assigned seats as allocated by the examination system.

When will my seat be available?

Seat allocations are typically published 3-5 days before the examination period. You will be notified via email and the announcements page once your seat is available.

What if I have two exams on the same day?

If you have multiple exams on the same day, you may be assigned different seats for each exam. Check your seat allocation for each examination separately.

Is my seat information secure?

Yes. Your seat information is only accessible to you after completing identity verification. Only you can view and access your examination details.
