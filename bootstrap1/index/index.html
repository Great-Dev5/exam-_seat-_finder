How It Works

Exam Seat Finder brings your examination information together in one convenient platform. Follow these simple steps to access your examination details quickly and securely.

1

Register Account

Create your student account and complete your registration. Ensure all your details are correct before proceeding.

2

Verify Identity

Complete the required biometric verification to confirm your identity. This ensures that only you can access your examination information.

3

Find Your Seat

View your assigned examination seat number, course details, date, time, and venue all in one place. You can also download your examination docket.

Important Notice

Make sure your examination requirements have been completed before checking your seat. This includes tuition payment, course registration, and biometric verification.
