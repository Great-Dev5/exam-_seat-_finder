Complete Guide to Using Exam Seat Finder

Exam Seat Finder is a comprehensive platform designed to help students access all their examination information quickly and easily. Whether you need to find your seat, check your timetable, or view examination venues, this platform provides everything you need in one place. Below is a detailed guide on how to use the Exam Seat Finder platform effectively.

Step 1: Student Registration

The first step to using Exam Seat Finder is to create your student account. All registered students of Ambrose Alli University are eligible to create an account. To register, you will need your student ID number and a valid email address. The registration process is simple and takes just a few minutes to complete.

  • Visit the Exam Seat Finder homepage and click on the "Sign Up" button.
  • Fill in the required information including your full name, student ID, email address, and password.
  • Verify your email address by clicking on the confirmation link sent to your inbox.
  • Once verified, you can log in to your account and begin accessing examination information.

Step 2: Biometric Verification

To ensure the security and integrity of the examination process, all students must complete biometric verification. This step is mandatory and must be completed before you can access any examination information. The biometric verification process involves capturing your fingerprints and facial recognition data.

  • Visit the university's designated biometric verification center on campus.
  • Present your student ID card and complete the biometric capture process.
  • Wait for the system to confirm that your biometric data has been successfully captured and stored.
  • You will receive a confirmation notification once the process is complete.

Step 3: Logging In

Once you have completed registration and biometric verification, you can log in to the Exam Seat Finder platform. Use your student ID and password to access your account. It is recommended that you change your password regularly for security purposes.

  • Go to the Exam Seat Finder homepage and click on the "Login" button.
  • Enter your student ID and password.
  • Click the "Login" button to access your dashboard.
  • If you have forgotten your password, use the "Forgot Password" link to reset it.

Step 4: Finding Your Seat

Once logged in, you can easily find your allocated examination seat. The system automatically assigns seats based on your course registration and examination schedule.

  • Navigate to the "Find My Seat" page from the main menu.
  • Enter your student ID or matriculation number to retrieve your seat information.
  • Your seat number, examination hall, date, and time will be displayed on the screen.
  • You can also download your examination docket for printing.

Step 5: Viewing Your Timetable

The timetable feature provides a comprehensive schedule of all your examinations. You can view your examinations by date, course, or venue.

  • Navigate to the "Timetable" page from the main menu.
  • Select your course or date range to filter the schedule.
  • View the list of examinations including dates, times, courses, and venues.
  • You can also export your timetable to a printable format.

Step 6: Checking Examination Venues

If you are unsure about the location of your examination hall, you can view detailed information about all examination venues on campus.

  • Navigate to the "Examination Venues" page from the main menu.
  • View the list of all examination halls with their details.
  • Each venue includes information on capacity, facilities, and location.
  • You can also view a map showing the location of each venue.

Step 7: Staying Informed with Announcements

The announcements section keeps you updated with the latest information regarding your examinations. This includes schedule changes, important notices, and emergency updates.

  • Navigate to the "Announcements" page from the main menu.
  • View the list of all announcements and updates.
  • Click on individual announcements to read more details.
  • Enable notifications to receive updates directly on your dashboard.

Step 8: Managing Your Profile

Your profile section allows you to manage your personal information and account settings. Ensure that your details are always up to date.

  • Navigate to the "My Profile" page from the main menu.
  • View and update your personal information.
  • Change your password for security purposes.
  • Update your contact information including email and phone number.
Need Help? If you encounter any issues while using the Exam Seat Finder platform, please contact the examination office or visit the student support center for assistance.
