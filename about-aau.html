About Ambrose Alli University

Discover the history, mission, and excellence of AAU, Ekpoma.

About Ambrose Alli University

Ambrose Alli University (AAU), Ekpoma, is a state-owned university located in Ekpoma, Edo State, Nigeria. The university was established in 1981 by Professor Ambrose Folorunsho Alli, who was the Governor of the old Bendel State at the time. The university's motto is "Knowledge for Advancement."

The institution's name has changed over time to reflect its history. It was originally founded as Bendel State University in 1981. In 1992, it was renamed Edo State University. Finally, it was named Ambrose Alli University in honor of its founder, Professor Ambrose Folorunsho Alli, who was a visionary leader and educationist.

The university's main campus is situated along the Benin-Auchi Road in Ekpoma. Its permanent site was established on a large parcel of land provided by the local community. Over the years, the university has grown significantly with newer buildings, including a complex for the Faculty of Life Sciences completed in 2018. The campus continues to expand with modern facilities to accommodate the growing student population.

Academic Structure

AAU offers a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs across 14 faculties. Some of its prominent faculties include:

  • Faculty of Arts: Offers programs in English, History and International Studies, Modern Languages (e.g., French), Philosophy, Religious and Cultural Studies, and Theatre and Media Arts.
  • Faculty of Physical Sciences: Includes departments like Mathematics and Statistics, which offer specialized degrees in Mathematics, Industrial Mathematics, and Statistics.
  • Faculty of Engineering: Provides professional degrees in fields like Civil Engineering, with a curriculum designed to combine theoretical foundations with practical skills.
  • Other Faculties: The university also has faculties dedicated to Agriculture, Law, Management Sciences, Social Sciences, Medicine, and Clinical Sciences, among others.

The institution is fully accredited and its programs are recognized by the National Universities Commission (NUC), ensuring that students receive quality education that meets national and international standards.

Leadership and Recent Developments

The current acting Vice-Chancellor is Professor Asomwan Sonnie Adagbonyin, who was appointed in February 2022. Under his leadership, the university has seen significant progress and development. In recent news, the university management has publicly thanked the Edo State Government for increasing investment in the institution, which has helped resolve previous issues and allowed for progress, including the induction of medical and nursing students.

The university continues to make strides in academic excellence, research, and community development. It remains committed to producing graduates who are not only academically sound but also equipped with the skills and values needed to contribute meaningfully to society.

Notable Alumni

AAU has produced many distinguished alumni who have made significant contributions in various fields. A few notable names include:

  • Aisha Buhari: Wife of the former president of Nigeria, known for her philanthropic work and advocacy for women and children.
  • Tony Elumelu: A prominent Nigerian investor, philanthropist, and entrepreneur who is the founder of the Tony Elumelu Foundation.
  • Don Jazzy: Renowned singer, music producer, and entrepreneur who has made significant contributions to the Nigerian music industry.
  • Festus Keyamo: A Senior Advocate of Nigeria (SAN), human rights activist, and politician who has been instrumental in various legal and political developments in Nigeria.

These alumni serve as role models and ambassadors of the university, showcasing the quality of education and character instilled at AAU.
