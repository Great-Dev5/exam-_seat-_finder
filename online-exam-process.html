Online Exam Process

Understanding the complete online examination procedure at AAU.

Online Examination Process at AAU

The online examination process at Ambrose Alli University is designed to provide students with a seamless and efficient way to access their examination information. The system is built to ensure that every student can easily find their examination seat, venue, date, and time without any hassle.

The process begins with student registration, where each student is assigned a unique matriculation number and student ID. These credentials are used to access the Exam Seat Finder portal, which serves as the central hub for all examination-related information.

1. Student Registration

Every student is required to complete their registration at the beginning of each academic session. This includes providing accurate personal information, uploading a passport photograph, and completing any necessary documentation. The registration process ensures that each student's identity is verified and linked to their unique student ID and matriculation number.

Students are also required to provide a valid email address and phone number for communication purposes. This information is used to send important updates regarding examination schedules, seat allocations, and other relevant announcements.

2. Biometric Verification

To ensure the security and integrity of the examination process, all students are required to complete biometric verification. This involves capturing fingerprints and facial recognition data, which are stored securely in the university's database. The biometric verification process helps prevent impersonation and ensures that only the rightful student can access their examination information.

Students who fail to complete biometric verification will not be able to access the Exam Seat Finder portal or their examination details. It is therefore crucial that all students complete this process well before the examination period begins.

3. Seat Allocation

Once registration and biometric verification are completed, students are assigned examination seats. The seat allocation process is automated and ensures that every student is assigned a seat in the appropriate examination hall. The allocation takes into account the student's course, department, and faculty to ensure that students are seated in the correct venue.

Students can view their allocated seat numbers through the Exam Seat Finder portal. The seat number is displayed alongside other important information such as the examination date, time, and venue. Students are advised to check their seat allocation at least 24 hours before the examination to avoid any last-minute confusion.

4. Accessing Examination Information

The Exam Seat Finder portal allows students to access all their examination information in one place. By logging in with their credentials, students can view:

  • Examination Timetable: A complete schedule of all examinations, including dates, times, and course codes.
  • Seat Number: The specific seat assigned to the student for each examination.
  • Venue Details: The location of the examination hall, including building name and room number.
  • Examination Rules: Important rules and regulations that students must follow during the examination.
  • Announcements: Any updates or changes related to the examination schedule or process.

5. Examination Day Process

On the day of the examination, students are expected to arrive at the examination hall at least 30 minutes before the scheduled start time. Students must present their student ID card and examination docket for verification before being allowed entry into the hall.

Once inside the hall, students are required to sit in their assigned seats as indicated on the Exam Seat Finder portal. Any deviation from the assigned seat may result in disqualification or other penalties. Students are also expected to comply with all examination rules and regulations during the examination.

6. Post-Examination Process

After the examination, the university follows a structured process for marking, grading, and result publication. Students can view their results through the university's official portal. The Exam Seat Finder portal may also be used to notify students of their results once they are published.

Students are encouraged to provide feedback on the examination process through the university's feedback channels. This helps the university identify areas for improvement and ensure that the examination process remains efficient and effective for all students.

Important: Students are advised to regularly check the Exam Seat Finder portal for updates and announcements. Any changes to the examination schedule or process will be communicated through the portal.

The online examination process at AAU is continuously evolving to meet the needs of students and to incorporate new technologies. The university remains committed to providing a fair, transparent, and efficient examination system that supports academic excellence and student success.
